The emerging thesis

I think we can now describe the platform in one sentence:

A guided, local-first platform that helps business owners define their ownership-transition goals, explore potential paths, assemble the right professional team, and produce actionable Professional Review Packages for qualified advisors to validate and execute.

And the seller-note marketplace, professional marketplace, financing coordination, local document vault, and transaction workflow all sit underneath that central idea.

The owner brings the goal.

The platform organizes the goal.

Professionals determine the path.

The transaction team executes it.

That is a very clean foundation to build from.

The distinction is powerful:

We are not the professional advisor. We are the owner's preparation, coordination, and translation layer.

🔒 Locked Product Principle: Professional Review Packages

The platform will not:

Draft legal contracts
Provide legal advice
Provide tax advice
Provide investment advice
Make professional valuation conclusions
Provide lending decisions
Act as the owner's attorney, CPA, investment advisor, trustee, or other regulated professional

Instead, it will:

Capture → Organize → Model → Consolidate → Communicate

the owner's objectives and preferences.

The output is a Professional Review Package that says, essentially:

"Here is what I am trying to accomplish. Here is what matters to me. Here are the paths I've explored. Here are the assumptions I've provided. Here are the trade-offs I understand. Here are the questions I need you, as my professional advisor, to validate or change."

That is a very different product from an AI advisor.

The package becomes the bridge between the owner and the professionals

Imagine the owner completes the journey and receives:

Owner's primary objective

Secondary objectives

Significant cash at closing

Open to staged transition

Questions for advisors

Which structures are legally feasible?
Which structures best align with the owner's stated goals?
What valuation work is required?
What financing capacity exists?
What are the tax implications?
What specialized professionals are required?
What assumptions need to change?

Status

🟡 Preliminary owner-directed plan requiring professional review

That document becomes the common starting point for the CPA, attorney, valuation professional, lender, trustee, and other specialists.

And there is an even better implication

The package shouldn't be static.

Think of it as a living document.

Version 1

Owner's Initial Objectives

↓

Professional reviews it

↓

Version 2

Owner + Professional Revised Plan

↓

Another professional reviews it

↓

Version 3

Validated Transaction Direction

↓

Transaction execution begins

So the platform can preserve the decision history without pretending to be the decision-maker.

That gives everyone a common reference point:

What did the owner originally want? What changed? Why did it change? Who advised the change?

That could become extremely valuable in complicated transactions.

I would add one more rule

The platform should distinguish between three things throughout the entire experience:

Layer	Meaning
Owner Objective	What the owner wants
Platform Scenario	What might potentially accomplish it under stated assumptions
Professional Determination	What the qualified professional concludes should actually be done

Those three should never get blended together.

That separation could become one of the defining characteristics of the product.
